Ndustrial

AI energy intelligence for industrial clients
Role
Sole designer. Set up the design and product process from scratch. Owned research through delivery, plus design ops and strategy.
Challenge
An AI recommending energy curtailments, used by plant operators, not data scientists. The hard part was trust, not visualization. If it reads as a black box, they stop using it.
$100k
a wrong call, one facility, one month
200+
industrial sites running it

The AI surfaces, the operator decides

Approach
Show what it found, how confident it is, and the evidence. Then get out of the way. Every recommendation has a visible "why" and an override, so the operator always holds the final call.
Outcome
Operator surfaces live across 200+ industrial sites. A reusable model for the human and AI division of labor.

Brightly / Siemens

Role
Built the design function from a small group into a team of seven, including a Principal running three of her own designers. About 7.5 years.
Challenge
12 products from acquisitions, 4 markets, each on its own siloed dataset. None talking to each other. For a multi-product customer it felt like software from separate companies. All during a rebrand, a $1.575B acquisition, and Covid.

Brightly / Siemens

Approach
Two layers. People: career ladders and mentorship matter, but they rest on respecting each person's autonomy and what they want from the work. Platform: a north-star vision for running all 12 products on one connected, microserviced data layer, so a customer gets the whole job done in one place.
Outcome
Lowest attrition in the company through it all. The vision was compelling enough to go into the acquisition pitch to Siemens.

Epilog

An app for a loved one with epilepsy
Role
Designer and builder, solo, end to end.
Challenge
A log on its own tells you nothing. Existing trackers record events but never reveal the pattern. The real problem was surfacing the correlations hidden across dozens of life factors, the ones no person could spot, and making the data count in the neurologist's office.
dozens of signals, logged daily. no visible pattern.

Logging tool, to calendar, to AI pattern-finder

Approach
The app grew from a logging tool, to a calendar, to an AI pattern-finder. I fed it sleep, heart rate, and activity from a smartwatch plus logged meds, diet, and notes, then let it find the correlations a person scanning a calendar would miss, and cross-reference medical research for other factors worth tracking.
Outcome
The AI surfaced a likely supplement and medication interaction nobody had connected. The neurologist confirmed it, the seizures eased, and a human made the call.
highlighted: a single supplement change, and the window after it where every seizure occurred.
